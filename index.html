The Visionary Behind Integra

A seasoned medical executive and cardiovascular specialist, Dr. Maureen Omolola Chukwura founded Integra to address the critical need for integrated preventive cardiology and holistic wellness in Nigeria.

Her approach combines state-of-the-art cardiac care with lifestyle medicine, nutrition, stress management, and personalised health coaching — empowering patients to prevent disease and optimise vitality. Under her leadership, Integra offers a unique one-stop model for heart health and overall wellness, serving professionals and executives seeking proactive, high-quality care.
