An ECG measures the electrical signals that control the timing of each heartbeat. At Integra, every ECG is performed by trained clinical staff and interpreted by our Cardiovascular Specialist. It is the gold-standard first-line investigation for detecting a wide range of cardiac conditions, from rhythm disturbances to signs of a prior heart attack.

What's included

  • 12-lead heart rhythm recording across standard limb and precordial leads
  • Full skin preparation and precise electrode placement by trained cardiac staff
  • Real-time trace capture with immediate quality verification
  • Preliminary interpretation and annotated written report by a cardiac specialist

Why it matters

  • Non-invasive, painless and completed in under 15 minutes
  • Immediate detection of arrhythmias, conduction defects and ischaemic changes
  • Specialist interpretation, not just an automated reading
  • Included in every Executive Wellness package

What to expect

You lie comfortably while small adhesive electrodes are placed on your chest, arms and legs. The machine records your heart's electrical activity over roughly 10 seconds. Results print immediately and are reviewed by our cardiovascular specialist, with a written report and, where indicated, a recommendation for further tests or a follow-up consultation.

Echocardiography uses high-frequency sound waves to produce live, moving images of your heart, revealing its chambers, valves, walls and connected vessels. At Integra this is performed and interpreted by our specialist physician, providing insights no other single test can match, essential for diagnosing valve disease, cardiomyopathy, heart failure and congenital defects.

What's included

  • High-resolution 2D transthoracic ultrasound of all four cardiac chambers
  • Colour and spectral Doppler flow analysis across all four heart valves
  • Regional and global wall-motion assessment for ischaemia detection
  • Ejection fraction, chamber dimensions and diastolic function, with a full written report

Why it matters

  • Detailed visualisation of all four heart chambers and valves
  • Accurate measurement of cardiac function and ejection fraction
  • Detects structural abnormalities, fluid around the heart and wall-motion defects
  • No radiation, completely safe and repeatable

What to expect

You lie on your left side on an examination couch. A small amount of gel is applied to the chest and the probe is gently moved across the skin for roughly 30-45 minutes. Our specialist walks you through key findings during the session, and a full written echo report follows with clinical implications discussed at a dedicated consultation.

From routine electrocardiography to advanced ambulatory monitoring, every test is interpreted personally by our specialist to ensure an accurate diagnosis and a care plan tailored to your unique cardiovascular profile.

What's included

  • 24/48-hour continuous ambulatory ECG (Holter) via a lightweight wearable monitor
  • Full device setup, skin preparation and patient diary instruction in-clinic
  • Automated arrhythmia detection with expert manual over-read of the full recording
  • Detailed Holter report plus a specialist review consultation

Why it matters

  • Early detection of arrhythmias, ischaemia and structural abnormalities
  • Personalised risk stratification and management plans
  • Reduced risk of acute cardiac events through proactive monitoring
  • Continuity of care with specialist-led follow-up

What to expect

Your visit opens with a thorough history and clinical assessment. Depending on your risk profile you may have an ECG, Holter fitting, Echocardiography, ambulatory BP monitoring, or a Cardiac Stress Test. Results are discussed in a follow-up consultation with a written report and a personalised action plan.

A 1-day course delivering AHA and RCA-aligned Basic Life Support training for healthcare professionals and lay responders, run in partnership with Code.Red Emergency Medical Services, an American Heart Association Authorized Training Center.

What's included

  • AED operation and choking relief (Heimlich and back blows)
  • Two-rescuer CPR coordination and BVM ventilation
  • Internationally recognised certification card, valid 2 years

Why it matters

  • Globally recognised certification
  • Builds muscle memory for the critical first 3-5 minutes
  • Meets mandatory requirements for workplaces and schools
  • Small-group, hands-on format

What to expect

A 6-10 hour course blending theory and practical skill stations, closing with a written knowledge check and an observed skills assessment. Certification is issued on the day.

A 3-day course for doctors, nurses, paramedics and senior allied health professionals requiring mastery of advanced resuscitation, covering complex cardiac arrest algorithms, advanced airway techniques and emergency pharmacology.

What's included

  • Advanced airway management: ETT, LMA, i-gel
  • 12-lead ECG interpretation of shockable and non-shockable rhythms
  • Emergency pharmacology: adrenaline, amiodarone, atropine
  • Full ALS algorithms and team-leadership debrief

Why it matters

  • Internationally recognised ALS / ACLS certification
  • Systematic ECG interpretation and advanced airway mastery
  • Evidence-based pharmacology under pressure
  • Sharper team coordination during resuscitation

What to expect

Three full days with escalating simulation scenarios: day one covers theory and skills, days two and three are scenario-intensive, managing full resuscitations. Assessment includes a written exam and a summative megacode.
